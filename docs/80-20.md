# 80/20 scan

The page was arguing the same case in too many rooms. The 20% that does the work is: what it is, why the street and the class gap, who walks in, why this side of the city, the rooms, how members act, the week, the twelve weeks, the ask.

Everything else was a second thesis, a second belonging beat, or proof that did not match the claim.

## Spine (kept)

1. **Hero.** Tools, mentors, and opportunity in the hands of young men who grew up without them.
2. **What is Life Row?** One line: East LA, youth and young adults, resources limited by income. Display size lives here.
3. **Why we exist.** The street. Income should not be the barrier. What kids from money get.
4. **Who we’re here for.** The young man, including probation, gangs, conflict at home, a safe place away from home. Why young men.
5. **Why here.** Arts District, Boyle Heights, the 10. West side resources belong in everyday life here. Role models and business owners from this side of the city.
6. **What access means.** The table only. The list is the proof.
7. **What we stand on.** Stewardship and Discipleship. Those do not appear anywhere else.
8. **How we live.** Value / principle. Five acts, from day one.
9. **One community.** Twelve at a time, ages 10 to 21, four cohorts a year.
10. **Weekly experience.** Day, time, age group.
11. **Twelve weeks.** The path.
12. **Help us build it.** Time or funding, interest list, partner.

## Left on purpose (not the same beat)

- **Hero vs Access.** Hero is the work sentence. Access is the inventory.
- **Why we exist vs Access.** Why exist names the class gap, including better schools. Access names what this house actually runs. Those should not be collapsed until you decide whether “better schools” stays.
- **How we live vs Twelve weeks.** One is how they treat each other from day one. One is the quarter.
- **Who vs Why we exist.** Street is the wound. Who is who walks in.
- **Stewardship and Discipleship.** House beliefs, not member practices.

## Still unresolved (not redundancy)

- What is says youth and young adults. The rest of the page says young men. Ages start at 10.
- Four cohorts a year vs one class of twelve on Sunday. Intake math is still unclear.
- A 10 year old and an 18 to 21 year old share a 12 week path and a Sunday room. No word on how that is run.
- Better schools is named in Why we exist and not in Access.
- Faith is a principle. No word on whether it is required.
- Interest list still says eligibility and dates come later. The week already reads as a timetable.
